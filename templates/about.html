A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you go.

Are these real bookings?

No. The planner produces estimated costs and research. No tickets or rooms are purchased.

Can I change a trip later?

Yes. Your browser remembers the current trip. Reopen the planner to change the length, budget, or disruption scenario.

What happens when plans change?

Ask for rain on a specific day or a flight delay. The planner replaces or removes affected stops and calculates the budget again.

What powers the plan?

The server uses OpenAI for itinerary ordering and the trip report. Flight and hotel figures are estimated allowances, not live fares or verified availability.
